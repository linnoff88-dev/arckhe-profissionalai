🧑‍💻 Become an AI Person

You don’t need to be the world’s best AI engineer. You just need to know more than your circle—it’s relative, and it opens doors before a formal role exists. This module shows you how to become “the AI person” on your team without changing careers.

🎯 “AI person” is relative

Being “the AI person” no means mastering all the models and the architecture behind them. It means knowing more than the people around you—colleagues, family, team. It's a measure relative to your circle, not an absolute title. The person who made this video "feels overwhelmed every day by how much they still don't know"—and is still the AI go-to in their group.

🧠 The central idea

Someone probably already sees you as the “AI person.” If not, that’s exactly who you want to become—in your own context.

  • •It’s not about being the best in the world; it’s about being the go-to person for your group.
  • •The feeling of “I don’t know enough” is universal — don’t let it paralyze you.
  • •Today, this is an advantage; in a few years, it will be the new normal. The window is now.

🚪 How This Opens Doors

Companies will have many “AI moments”: access to a new model, an internal product, an AI task force. When someone says "we need someone to lead this", you want someone else to answer: "I know someone who'd be into this". That’s how opportunities show up— before of having a formal job title.

🛠️ Choose 1 Tool and Get Good at It

The practical move: take a main tool and get really good at it. The exact tool isn’t the point — the point is to stop “playing around” and start deliver ROI with it. Depth with one tool beats spreading yourself across ten.

✓ What to DO

  • ✓Choose 1 tool and use it every day for a real task.
  • ✓Measure the outcome: what did it really save you?
  • ✓Explore advanced features before moving on.

✗ What NOT to do

  • ✗Jumping from tool to tool without mastering any of them.
  • ✗Just “testing” without producing anything worthwhile.
  • ✗Confusing collecting apps with getting results.

💡 Practical tip

For knowledge work and automation, the method’s author uses the Claude as your primary tool. You can choose yours — what matters is the rule: a tool, used to deliver real value, not ten open in tabs.

🔁 Choose 1 Workflow from Your Work

Pick something you already does every week and discover how to use AI to do it better or faster. Don’t change careers — find the AI-native version of the career you already have. The question is simple: “how can I get faster, better, and more useful at this specific task with AI?”

1

List your weekly tasks

Write down what keeps recurring: reports, emails, spreadsheets, summaries, proposals.

2

Choose 1 with a clear pain point

The tedious, time-consuming task you’d love to shorten. Start with that.

3

Rebuild with AI

"3 hours → 20 minutes" is the kind of leap that makes a story worth telling.

📈 Document before and after

What turns an experiment into reputation is showing. Document how long it took before, how long it takes now, what improved, and what still required human judgment. Then show someone — "look what I built this weekend." That's almost always all it takes for you to become "the AI person."

🛡️ Be Smart: Data, Permission, and Rules

Being the AI person also means being responsible. Don’t expose company data, violate regulations, or automate anything at work without permission. And if your company prohibits a tool? That is another opportunity: find out what rules exist and which tools you can actually use—that alone shows you care.

⚠️ Attention

Pasting sensitive client/company data into a public tool may violate policy and law. When in doubt, anonymize it, ask legal/security, and prefer approved tools. Reputation is built slowly and lost quickly.
