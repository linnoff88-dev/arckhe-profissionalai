🧠 Context Engineering & Your AI OS

🔀 Prompt × Context

They’re two different things — and confusing them is the most common mistake. A prompt is how you ask: the wording, the tone, the instruction of the moment. Context is what AI knows: your business, your history, your priorities, the document you attached. The prompt disappears when the conversation ends; the context stays.

The model is the same for everyone; the context is yours — illustrative diagram.

⏳ Why context is more durable

Models improve every month — and each leap makes part of “prompt engineering” obsolete. But no leap solves one thing: the model doesn’t know what’s in your head. Your history, your decisions, the way your company works. That’s why context is the step that doesn’t evaporate: you invest in it once, and it pays off with every future model.

📊 What each model leap changes — and what it doesn’t

When a new model comes out, yesterday's magic phrase may become unnecessary. What no what changes is how much your specific knowledge is needed to produce something relevant.

  • →A better model = less prompt trickery needed.
  • →A better model = the same dependence on your context.
  • →Context invested today pays off with tomorrow’s model.

🚫 Stop Opening a Blank Chat

Most people start a new conversation from scratch every time—and spend half their time re-explaining who they are, what they do, and what they want. Stop. Use projects, GPTs, and persistent assistants: add your documents, calendar, and priorities once, then reuse them in every conversation. You stop being a stranger with every new chat.

  1. 1

    Create a persistent project

    Instead of scattered chats, open a “project” that stores instructions and files.

  2. 2

    Inject your documents

    Procedures, examples of your best work, glossary, house rules.

  3. 3

    Add calendar and priorities

    What’s happening in your week, what matters now.

  4. 4

    Reuse, don’t start over

    Every new conversation already starts knowing who you are.

🗄️ The AI-OS: Everything in Your Head

The next step is to build your AI-OS: a living repository of everything you know and produce. Meeting transcripts, emails, DMs, videos, notes, decisions. When AI has access to this, it starts to know you better than you remember — because it doesn’t forget what you said six months ago, and you do.

🧑‍🎓 The Summer Intern Analogy

Think of AI as a brilliant summer intern, but one that arrived today. Without onboarding, it's clever—but it guesses. It doesn't know your clients, your tone, or your rules. You wouldn't fire the intern for that; you'd would train. Giving AI context is exactly that: onboarding that turns guesswork into useful work.

💡 Practical tip

Before complaining that "the AI didn’t understand," ask: would I give this same task to a first-day intern without explaining anything? If not, the problem isn’t AI—it’s the onboarding that was missing. Write the brief you’d give a new person.

♻️ Garbage in, garbage out—your IP is the differentiator

The final rule: garbage in, garbage out. Poor or missing context produces generic output — just like anyone else’s. Rich context, your real knowledge, your way of doing things — produces something only you can create. Your IP (intellectual property) is the differentiator. AI doesn’t replace what’s in your head; it amplifies it.

✓ Rich context (your IP)

  • ✓Examples of your best work as a reference.
  • ✓Your tone, your rules, the reason behind your decisions.
  • ✓Unique output that no one else can generate.

✗ Garbage in

  • ✗Blank chat, zero context about you.
  • ✗Outdated or contradictory documents.
  • ✗Generic output — just like everyone else’s.
