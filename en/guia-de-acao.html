✅ The 6 skills—checklist

  • 1 · Become the AI person. Choose 1 tool and get good at it; become the go-to person in your circle.
  • 2 · Taste & judgment. Don’t trust the 1st output; decide what deserves your name.
  • 3 · Context engineering. Stop opening blank chats; give AI what only you know.
  • 4 · Iteration speed. Build a fast, ugly prototype; iterate; know when to stop (define "done").
  • 5 · Build your Jarvis. An agent only when needed; a simple workflow when that’s enough.
  • 6 · Protection & multiple income streams. Multiple income streams from a single passion; build in public.

🗓️ 30-60-90 Onboarding Plan

0–30 days · Learn

  • Choose 1 primary tool.
  • Choose 1 weekly workflow of yours.
  • Redo it with AI every day.

31–60 days · Measure

  • Record before and after.
  • Show the team.
  • Become the go-to person on the subject.

61–90 days · Scale

  • Automate a 2nd workflow.
  • Build your mini AI-OS.
  • Open up 1 income stream.

🤖 Agent × Workflow — The Quick Test

Workflow (vending machine)

Deterministic task, same input → same output. E.g.: "every day at 9 a.m., pull the revenue and post it to Slack". Inexpensive, doesn’t break.

Agent (slot machine)

Messy input, requires reasoning, and generates content. Example: “read these emails and draft replies.” Powerful, more expensive, and riskier.

Always ask: (1) Do I need to trigger this, or does the system do it on its own? (2) does this need AI, or will a simple script do the job?
